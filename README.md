# Development Working Group

The Development Working Group, or Dev WG for short, focuses on various
development topics surrounding OpenBao. Its purpose and governance is outlined
in the dedicated [charter](./CHARTER.md).

- Lead: Alex Scheel ([@cipherboy](https://github.com/cipherboy))
- Mailing list: [openbao-dev-wg@lists.openssf.org](https://lists.lfedge.org/g/openbao-dev-wg)
- Zulip: https://linuxfoundation.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/529890-openssf-openbao-discussion/topic/dev-wg.20.28interim.29/with/560343271

The subgroups use dedicated and private mailing lists. A more standardized
collaboration approach (e.g. meeting minutes or issue management) is currently
under revision.

To join a mailing list, please reach out to the working group lead and
they'll get you added or email `<list>+subscribe@lists.openssf.org`.
